# Product — Quine Systems

## Register

**Brand** — A digital design studio landing page where the interface itself is the primary sales surface and portfolio.

## Users

Founders, business owners, and ventures (primarily in tech, lifestyle, and finance) evaluating whether to engage a design studio for brand and digital presence work.

Their context: browsing agency portfolios, making snap judgments about craft quality within seconds. The landing page must immediately communicate "this team understands design" without saying it explicitly.

The job to be done: convince a prospect that Quine can deliver a premium, memorable digital experience worth investing in.

## Product Purpose

Quine Systems is a digital design studio. Its landing page functions as both portfolio and product — the interface itself must sell the craft. Every element should demonstrate the studio's capability: typography, spacing, motion, and interaction quality must be evidence of expertise.

Success looks like a visitor feeling "this team gets it" within 5 seconds, then smoothly guided to make contact.

## Brand Personality

- **Unconventional yet functional** — surprising in small ways, never at the expense of usability
- **Minimal yet tastefully sophisticated** — restrained, never empty; every element earns its place
- **Intuitive and user-centric** — the experience feels effortless, never performative for its own sake

Emotional targets: **soft modernism**, **trustworthy analog**, **humanist**, **straightforward sincerity**. Nostalgic "clean and elegant yet trustworthy" feeling akin to classic Apple "Test drive a Macintosh" advertising from the late 1980s / early 1990s.

## Design Principles

1. **The interface IS the portfolio** — every pixel, transition, and typographic choice must demonstrate craft. Nothing is decorative filler.
2. **Restraint over excess** — minimal yet tastefully sophisticated. When in doubt, remove. What remains must be excellent.
3. **Analog warmth meets digital precision** — humanist and approachable, not cold or clinical. Trustworthy, not flashy.
4. **Unconventional without being confusing** — surprising details are welcome, but functionality always wins. Never sacrifice clarity for novelty.
5. **Evolve, don't overhaul** — the current page is a rough sketch. Refine incrementally: keep what works, change what doesn't.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Standard WCAG 2.1 AA target
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animated elements (scramble text, staggered reveals, shader backgrounds)
- Ensure sufficient contrast ratios across the warm beige / dark text palette
- Smooth-scroll anchors and clear focus states for keyboard navigation
